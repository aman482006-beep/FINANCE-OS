# Finance OS Product Roadmap

## Product principle
Finance OS is one continuous learning and practice environment. Learning, assessment, simulations, work samples and career readiness should share one learner profile and evidence history.

## Phase 0 — foundation (this repository bootstrap)
- Responsive terminal-style application shell and navigation.
- Starter curriculum across financial foundations, markets, accounting, valuation, portfolio management, macro, modelling and professional skills.
- Lesson completion persisted in the browser.
- Simulated order ticket with virtual cash, holdings and order history.
- News-analysis scenarios clearly labelled as hypothetical.
- Career track directory and starter valuation workbench.
- Progress snapshot and AI mentor integration boundary.

## Phase 1 — core learning platform
- Server-side authentication and profile settings.
- Structured content model with lesson content, examples, exercises, worked calculations and assessment rubrics.
- Onboarding assessment and editable learner roadmap.
- Server-side progress persistence, spaced repetition and skill evidence.
- AI mentor with Explain, Socratic, Challenge, Professional and Revision modes.
- Admin/editor workflows for curriculum updates.

## Phase 2 — practical application
- Licensed market data provider adapter and data timestamp/latency labels.
- Auditable simulator with order types, execution rules, fees, corporate actions and benchmark history.
- Trade rationale and post-trade reflection.
- News ingestion from permitted sources, source/date provenance, historical snapshots and post-event reveal.
- Company research dossier with filings and source-linked financials.

## Phase 3 — professional skills
- Three-statement, DCF, comparable-company, merger and LBO assignment templates.
- File upload/export and spreadsheet calculation engine.
- Rubric-driven work simulation and review workflows.
- Role-specific tracks, capstones and shareable work portfolio.

## Phase 4 — advanced intelligence
- Evidence-based learner skill model.
- Multi-agent Finance Council with explicit disagreement and source citations.
- Adaptive recommendations and AI mock interviews.
- Instructor dashboards and cohort analytics.

## Phase 5 — ecosystem
- Institutional controls, cohorts, competitions and partner programmes.
- Recruiter-facing portfolio sharing with user-controlled permissions.
- Additional geographies and licensed asset classes.

## Non-negotiable safeguards
- Never imply demo market values are live.
- Never route simulated orders to a broker.
- Clearly distinguish educational information from personalised investment advice.
- Store data provenance and timestamps for all financial facts.
- Skill ratings need observable evidence and transparent rubrics.
- AI output must expose assumptions, sources and uncertainty; financial calculations should be deterministic and tested.
- No job or investment-return guarantees.

## MVP acceptance criteria
- App installs and builds cleanly.
- Navigation works on desktop and mobile.
- Curriculum list and lesson completion work.
- Paper trading rejects invalid quantities, oversells and unaffordable buys.
- State survives refresh on the same browser.
- News cases are disclosed as simulations.
- No API secrets are shipped to the browser.
